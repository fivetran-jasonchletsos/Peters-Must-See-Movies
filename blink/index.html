Must See / Bonus — Doctor Who

Don't blink.

"I could have added the 17 DVDs in Doctor Who's Blink, but I have no idea what their titles are." — Pete Chletsos

In the 2007 episode Blink, the Doctor's prerecorded half of a conversation with Sally Sparrow appeared as Easter-egg commentary on 17 Doctor Who DVDs that were available at the time. Here is the best-effort canonical list.

  1. 01 / 171983

    The Five Doctors

    Multi-Doctor anniversary

    20th-anniversary special. The first four Doctors return (Hartnell via William Hurndall, Troughton, Pertwee, Tom Baker via archive). The reason every multi-Doctor story works.

  2. 02 / 171989

    The Curse of Fenric

    Seventh

    WWII naval base, ancient evil, Soviet commandos. McCoy and Aldred's strongest joint outing — the moment the show grew up just before it was cancelled.

  3. 03 / 171982

    Earthshock

    Fifth

    Cybermen return after seven years. Adric dies. One of the few times a companion stayed dead. The end credits roll silently in mourning.

  4. 04 / 171976

    The Hand of Fear

    Fourth

    Eldrad must live. Sarah Jane's departure — the goodbye that still hurts. Tom Baker at peak Tom Baker.

  5. 05 / 171975

    Pyramids of Mars

    Fourth

    Sutekh the Destroyer trapped in a pyramid. Hinchcliffe-Holmes gothic horror, the era when Doctor Who scared a generation under the sofa.

  6. 06 / 171977

    The Robots of Death

    Fourth

    Sand-mining ship, Voc robots, an Agatha Christie locked-room mystery in space. Chris Boucher's tightest script.

  7. 07 / 171977

    The Talons of Weng-Chiang

    Fourth

    Victorian London, Sherlock-by-way-of-Holmes (Robert Holmes), giant rats in the sewers. Possibly the show's most-cited single serial.

  8. 08 / 171981

    Logopolis

    Fourth

    Tom Baker's regeneration. The Master, the universe unraveling, the Watcher waiting at the radio telescope. The end of an era.

  9. 09 / 171984

    Resurrection of the Daleks

    Fifth

    The bleakest Davison story. Daleks board a prison ship; Tegan walks out at the end, sickened. The body count is a record.

  10. 10 / 171988

    Remembrance of the Daleks

    Seventh

    Back to Coal Hill School, where the show began in 1963. McCoy at his most strategic, the Daleks staircase moment, the Hand of Omega.

  11. 11 / 171984

    The Caves of Androzani

    Fifth

    Davison's regeneration story, and many fans' choice for the best classic-era serial. Robert Holmes script, Graeme Harper direction.

  12. 12 / 171982

    Castrovalva

    Fifth

    Davison's first full serial — recursive Escher city, the Master pulling strings. The TARDIS shedding rooms into eternity.

  13. 13 / 171967

    The Tomb of the Cybermen

    Second

    Patrick Troughton, Telos, the Cybermen emerging from honeycomb tombs. Lost for decades, rediscovered in Hong Kong, restored to glory.

  14. 14 / 171965

    The Time Meddler

    First

    First-ever pseudo-historical: Vikings, the Monk, history-as-it-could-have-been. Hartnell finally in his comedic register.

  15. 15 / 171965

    The Web Planet

    First

    Vortis, giant ant Zarbi, Menoptera moths, sound-mixed alien atmosphere. The show committing fully to the strangeness it could be.

  16. 16 / 171968

    The Mind Robber

    Second

    The TARDIS falls out of reality. Gulliver, Rapunzel, the Karkus. The most dream-logic serial classic Who ever made.

  17. 17 / 171983

    The Black Adder

    —

    Not Doctor Who at all. The first Blackadder series, Rowan Atkinson's medieval comedy of incompetence. The Easter egg's outlier — and the joke.

The exact 17-DVD list from the Blink Easter egg is debated. This is reconstructed from on-screen credits and fan listings. Pete can update.
